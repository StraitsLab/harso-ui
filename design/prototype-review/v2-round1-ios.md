# Weave iOS v2 — independent visual review

## Scope and method

Reviewed all **76 iOS PNGs** in `index.json` individually with `vision_analyze`: 38 Light and 38 Dark, each verified as 390×844. Additional chrome/text-stack crops and direct PNG color-run measurements were used to resolve contradictory first-pass observations. No Sketch document, PNG, source code, or external system was changed.

Benchmark is the supplied Apple iOS 27 kit measurements and the Settings / Messages / Notes / Files patterns named in the brief. No live Apple-app screenshots were provided or captured; this is not a side-by-side device test. Pixel coordinates below refer to the original 1× canvas; approximate text bounds are marked ≈. Raster inspection cannot establish touch bounds, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, scrolling behavior, or exact font family.

**Scoring uses the full rubric, not the vision backend’s compressed suggested scores.** 9 means native and convincing at a glance; 8 means finished with one nit; 7 means one visible unfinished component; 6 means mixed native/web-like construction. Nothing here warrants 1–5: there is no verified tofu, broken screen, or severe clipping. No 10 is awarded without an actual Apple-equivalent screenshot match. Equal Light/Dark scores are intentional: each was inspected separately and the retained defects are structural.

**Explicit non-defects:** the 346×62 floating capsule and its selected pill ARE iOS 27; flat materials, one accent and Inter content are allowed. Text Cancel/Create/Done can be native controls and are not rejected merely for being text, although the supplied kit’s symbol toolbar is the preferred fix target. Long two-line metadata may exceed 52px; 68px rows are not automatically broken. Intentional empty states and short documents do not need filler. Left-aligned destructive settings rows are not inherently invalid. The brief says 270px alerts while HIG-SPEC says 300px: width alone is not scored down.

## Cross-cutting findings / fix once

1. **Two-line text helper is internally center-aligned.** This is the most important repeated defect, not a generic “needs polish” judgment. Cropped account, billing and projects rows show short labels centered over longer values. Direct PNG bounds confirm Projects title starts vary x111 → x141 → x120 while its subtitle anchor is about x75; billing Usage is x87 and Plan x169. Set the nested text stack to leading alignment, not center; use x32 without icon and x75 with icon. Apply to project folders/work, artifact metadata, preference metadata, and profile subtitles. Home/project/recent conversation rows use a different, correctly leading-aligned treatment: do not regress them.
2. **Editable preferences lack visible control semantics.** General, Appearance, Voice, Notifications, Connections and Shortcuts need value+picker/disclosure treatment. Allocate the 10×17 chevron at x348, value ending x334, label x32, and a 52px row where one-line values fit. Do not indiscriminately add chevrons to static version/usage metadata. Device management needs an explicit route too.
3. **Sheet layering erases the home indicator.** PNG samples at x100–290/y820–840 are uniform sheet color on approval, new project, new routine, plan and share. Put system gesture chrome above the modal overlay: 134×5 at (128,831), bottom safe area 34px. This fixes ten screens. Do not infer button overlap just because the bar is absent: first-pass claims that all bottom buttons collide were not retained.
4. **Approval sheet uses a different grabber helper.** Its opaque center is x178–211/y471–475, with antialiasing around the ends (~36×5 overall), not the specified 60×4. Move to x165/y469 on the y464 sheet. New-project/routine sheets already have the larger grabber; do not shrink them.
5. **Changed-file rows need one shared shape.** Activity work’s zero-gap rounded rectangles create ~4px side notches. One 12px outer-radius container plus straight internal boundaries fixes both appearances.
6. **Share extension identity is not interchangeable with SF Symbols.** Wi-Fi is not AirDrop; an open book is not Notes. Use actual destination icon assets at native tile size. The generic presentation is a real Files mismatch, whereas flat material is not.
7. **SSO identity is missing.** Both sign-in appearances lack the Apple mark; use the official provider control rather than manually approximating its branding. Google deserves the same treatment. No unsupported contrast-ratio claim is made: a vision model asserting WCAG failure without sampling is not evidence.
8. **What is already working:** status54/nav44/large-title52 composition, capsule tab bar, consistent 16px outer insets, real project names/paths/numbers, useful empty states, darkening scrims, anchored menus, and readable document/code content. No retained scaffold-copy or missing-glyph finding. Do not replace these with an older iOS visual language.

## Evidence quality and verification

All source PNGs decoded successfully. Temporary vision-provider 503 capacity failures were retried; every one of the 76 entries ultimately received a successful full-image review. Supplemental crops sometimes returned inaccurate claimed crop dimensions; their numeric coordinates were not trusted without original-raster checks. Initial “clean” generic passes also missed centered text stacks, so final scores incorporate the targeted crops rather than copying vision scores blindly. Several suggested defects were rejected as unsupported or contrary to this brief: old UITabBar assumptions, mandatory centered Sign Out, universal 52px subtitle rows, arbitrary need to fill blank space, and guessed contrast failures.

The machine-readable sidecar `ios-review-data.json` contains one record per exact index entry, including frame, PNG, score and fix. Coverage and result totals are calculated, not counted mentally.
