## Cross-cutting

1. **Reference parity is the main weakness, not broken layout.** Explore’s arrow substitutes for its compass; ButtonGroup substitutes an envelope and loses icon selection; Dropdown lacks the workspace/person trigger; ThemeToggle lacks the compact control; SocialButton lacks the demonstrated black default. These are concrete component differences, not a demand to reproduce the entire fixture UI.
2. **State copy has been generalized too aggressively.** “Saving changes” is reused for destructive and social-authentication actions. The social pending states also lose provider identification. InputOtp invalid uses border color alone while continuing to display ordinary instructions. Use action-specific pending/error copy without changing control geometry unnecessarily.
3. **The textarea appears to inherit single-line vertical centering.** Both appearances and every textarea state put the first line near the middle. Top-align multiline content with a stable inset; retain the sound alignment of adjacent single-line controls.
4. **Both appearances generally share the same defects.** Dark mode is not a light surface accidentally recolored: inversion, selected marks, focus outlines and menu text remain coherent. The specific additional Light/Clean issue is LinkButton’s non-accent hover underline.
5. **No verified clipping/overflow or default gray layout-frame failure in this lane.** Initial crop-edge “clipping” suggestions were disproved by full-sheet inspection. The dark Switch on-thumb was rechecked in a crop and is correctly right-aligned. Today’s ring in DatePicker is explicitly required, not a second erroneous selection. ThemeToggle’s content-sized segments have a normal inset gap, not a clipped seam.
6. **Do not repair valid design choices based on generic heuristics.** Disabled contrast, static spinners, a single active range bubble, an unchanged menu down-chevron, numeric up/down arrows, and generic social marks were not penalized. Primary pills are permitted by the written direction; “everything must be a pill” is not.
7. **Appearance coverage limitation:** the assigned index exposes only Light/Clean and Dark/Clean; the master build brief asks for four appearances. No Cozy sheets were supplied in this lane, so their existence/quality is unverified—not inferred absent from the Sketch document and not charged repeatedly against these 38 sheets.
8. **Minor composition parity remains even among 8s.** LinkButton has no Local action example; Radio does not show the composed Everyone/Partners group; Switch omits its helper sentence; DatePicker does not show the web fixture’s range mode. These were not escalated to structural omissions where the explicit lane brief only requires the primitive/state demonstrated. CloseButton’s visible cross ink is smaller than its nominal icon box; screenshot-only evidence is insufficient to assert incorrect icon-box size.

**Disposition:** Do not sign off the whole Controls kit until the below-8 defects are corrected. The sheets scored 8 are individually usable visual specimens, not proof of symbol naming, constraints, interactivity, accessibility semantics or unrendered appearance completeness.
