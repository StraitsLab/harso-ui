Fix the common chip text layout before polishing downstream compositions. Crop inspection rejected earlier apparent progress-bar overflow: the bar is inside the card. 3. **Task/Plan are simplified substitutes, not full structural parity.** Real-kit Task includes agent attribution, draft artifact, notes and a decision gate; Plan includes details, notes and an audience choice. Their Sketch sheets are tidy but omit those structures. Because reference structure is an acceptance criterion, geometry alone cannot earn 8. 4. **Stepper line placement is shared between themes.** Both connectors sit at the left tangent of the approximately 8px dots instead of the centerline, about 4px off. Crop inspection confirms circular dots and two present connectors; do not fix imaginary ellipse/missing-line defects.
5. **Question single/multiple-choice differentiation needs tightening.** The round selected radio uses the same checkmark language as the square checkbox. Use the kit's radio-dot semantics while retaining the clear tonal selected row. Submitted content remaining readable is not itself evidence of a missing disabled state.
6. **Shell composition passes; do not confuse bottom padding with an undocked composer.** 1440 reads as a three-column workspace, 1024 as its intentionally collapsed rail layout, and 390 as a usable mobile conversation with a bottom-anchored input. Mobile OS chrome/home indicators are not required in a web viewport. There is no confirmed horizontal clipping or composer/content overlap. Shell snapshot parity remains unverified because the requested reference files are absent.
7. **Quiet appearance tokens are broadly coherent.** Dark sheets use genuinely dark surfaces with light content; selected rows are tonal, dividers are quiet/inset, and floating surfaces are rounded. Generic favicons, different component-specific radii, static shimmer, whitespace in full-width cards, and persistent pop-up ⇅ glyphs are not defects. No exact contrast-failure claim is made without pixel measurement.

Harso is the intended brand, not a typo. Invisible action hit areas cannot be established from bare glyphs; screenshots alone do not prove WCAG target-size failure.
