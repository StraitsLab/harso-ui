**Not yet approved as a complete lane.** The rebuild repairs most round-1 family substitutions, but full-screen templates still omit their lower management modules, and mobile data schemas have not converged with the references.

Reviewed every indexed Blocks/Charts sheet: **70 sheets (32 Blocks, 38 Charts)**, including the two intentionally separate HrManagementPhone sheets. Each target PNG was decoded successfully and individually inspected with vision_analyze. Matching desktop-light references were inspected for every original family, including lower portions of tall dashboard references; DataTable and HR phone-dark references additionally establish mobile schema/control requirements. Regional reinspection covered table mobile specimens, ambiguous chart/ring findings, dashboard charts/calendar and reference continuations. No Sketch calls, browser/network access, source edits or kit changes.

Scores are fresh, not increments from round 1: 8 = shippable with ≤1 minor nit; 7 = a clear defect; ≤6 = multiple/structural defects. Light and dark were individually checked and receive equal scores where the same issues occur. Approximate dimensions describe exported evidence, not measured Sketch layer bounds. Templates were judged as complete 1280×800 screen deliverables, not isolated cards; intentionally separate phone sheets are not leakage. Their enlarged export footprints are a composition/delivery warning, not proof of clipped text or layer overflow. Missing lower modules are independently established by full-reference comparison.

No numerical WCAG claims, runtime behavior, invisible hit-area claims or inferred financial/clinical correctness. Do not normalize the intentional raw zero/fractional fixture values. Generic favicons, illustrative names/values, absence of preview-harness controls and static animation are not scored as defects.

- **DataTable:** original desktop selection/sort/action defects are fixed, but their action/schema divergence survives in the mobile specimen (ellipsis instead of view; unrelated currency/avatar fields). These are newly specified residual completeness failures, not a claim that every missing module was individually listed in round 1.
- **Legend-to-mark fidelity:** most old wrong-colour mappings are repaired. Remaining distinction loss occurs in Area's two grey series and SleepScore's two grey segments. This is subtler than the old green-legend/blue-mark contradictions.

### Repaired
- Limits expansion/segmentation; progress disclosure and numbered steps; thinking dot matrix; password sign-in form; full event calendar; six-metric StatCards; nested TaskList; WebSearch execution timeline; questionnaire close/category/Previous controls.
- Dashboard domain navigation/actions and characteristic top/middle modules are substantially restored: finance Sankey/rings/portfolio; home hire carousel/earnings/revenue/contributions; HR recent hires/pipeline/radar/combo; marketing funnel/allocation/tabs/analytics; medical demographics/calendar/activity/score breakdown.

- Chart control/scale recovery is broad: period selectors, direct values/tiles, Combo secondary percentage axis, Orders fractional scale, Revenue comparison summary/toggle, Scatter titles/means and Sankey named/detail data are now present. Radar guide/tile omission is repaired, but plotted point markers are missing; Sankey data lists exist, but their inspect-arrow affordances do not.
