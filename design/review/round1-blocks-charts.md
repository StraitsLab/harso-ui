# Blocks and Charts — independent strict visual review

## Scope and calibration

Reviewed all 68 indexed PNGs: 30 Blocks sheets and 38 Charts sheets, Light/Clean and Dark/Clean only. Every target PNG was opened with vision_analyze; DataTable and HrManagement received additional regional inspection. All files decoded successfully. The matching desktop-light web reference was inspected for every one of the 34 families before scoring. Ten dashboard specimens were judged as full screens, not isolated cards. No Sketch scripts, source edits, browser or network activity were used.

Scores use the supplied production-kit rubric: 8 = shippable with at most one minor nit; 7 = one clear defect; 6 or lower = multiple or structural defects. Reference structure/control omissions are scored; illustrative data, generic logos and static animation are not. Equal light/dark defects receive equal scores rather than rewarding dark-mode styling for identical structural omissions. Screenshot contrast was assessed visually only: no numeric WCAG pass/fail is claimed. Measurements are approximate; crop-edge cuts are not treated as actual clipping.

## Cross-cutting

1. **The main blocker is structural fidelity, not general polish.** Most surfaces have orderly spacing, consistent rounded containers and readable primary text. But Calendar becomes a date picker; TaskList an ordinary checklist; WebSearch a results page; MostActiveDays a bar chart; Radial a donut. These substitutions fail the stated web-kit structure requirement even where attractive in isolation.
2. **Full-screen templates repeat a generic shell instead of the five distinct applications.** The shared Overview/Activity/Records/Reports/Settings navigation and Export action replace domain-specific routes and actions. Finance, HR, marketing and medical screens omit their characteristic analytic or profile modules; Home omits the customer/hire/contributions structure. This is more consequential than padding refinements.
3. **Charts lose controls and inspectable values systematically.** Period selectors are absent or converted to static subtitles; per-series/per-category values and scale guidance are often dropped. A title, generic plot and pill legend are not sufficient replacements for the reference chart interface. Missing controls are visible specimen omissions, not claims about runtime interaction.
4. **Legend colours are not reliably bound to marks.** BarList stage pills imply green/amber while bars remain blue; Combo conversion is green in its key but blue in the plot; Contributions/Heatmap More is green on a blue-only intensity map; Steps Goal is green in the key but amber in the plot; StageBars leaves amber unexplained. The issue is inconsistent mapping, not the existence of semantic green/amber.
5. **Some chart transformations undermine interpretation.** Combo drops the secondary percentage axis; Orders retains fractional summaries while giving its plot a 0–100 unlabeled-normalization scale; Earnings adds a second stacked series; Area is not actually stacked; StageBars widths fail to express their printed percentages. These are encoding defects, not objections to fictional datasets.
6. **Light/dark parity is generally good.** Dark surfaces are actually dark, with light primary text and coherent surface separation. The structural defects persist across both appearances. No Cozy sheets belong to this index subset, and Cozy green accents were not scored.
7. **Actual composition leakage is concentrated in HR.** Both HR exports contain a mobile directory below the full-screen template, extending the sheet to 1344×1493 rather than the other full-screen sheets’ 1344×912. The complete DataTable sheet intentionally shows desktop and mobile specimens and is not penalized simply for that; its cropped sixth row was verified to continue in the original image.
8. **Selection-state fidelity needs attention.** DataTable shows one checked row and a correct selected-count footer, but the select-all box is still empty rather than partial. AgentProgress loses step numbers and expansion control; Questionnaire loses dismiss/category navigation. These are concrete specimen-state gaps.
